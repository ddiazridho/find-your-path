# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
High school students exploring suitable university majors.

## Product Purpose
Find Your Path helps students discover their interests, strengths, and preferences through real-world task simulations, with personalized AI insights to inform study choices.

## Capabilities and Constraints
This implementation is a frontend prototype in an existing Next.js project. A backend is planned. Find Your Path navigation uses #. Major cards open a popup; the final popup content and simulation flow are undecided. Use reusable global CSS. No layout attachment was available during implementation; the exploration-screen structure is an explicitly marked assumption pending user feedback.

## Brand Commitments
Name: FYP (Find Your Path). The user delegates the theme, buttons, icons, and logo design for a hackathon presentation.

## Evidence on Hand
PROJECT.md and the user brief. Major tasks are illustrative preview content, not live simulations or personalized recommendations.
